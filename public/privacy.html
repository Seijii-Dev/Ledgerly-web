Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Ledgerly is a personal expense tracker available as an Android app and on the web at https://ledgerly-web-g6.netlify.app/. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Ledgerly app and website.

Our commitment: We do not sell your personal information. Ledgerly does not use advertising SDKs, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. For online accounts, authentication and password handling are provided by Supabase. If you sign in with Google, Google shares basic account information such as your name, email address, and possibly a profile image URL. We do not receive or store your Google password.

Expense and budget information. We store the information you enter to use Ledgerly, including expense amounts, categories, payment methods, descriptions, dates, budgets, savings goals, custom categories, and related account settings.

Receipts and profile images. Receipt photos and profile or banner images you add are stored locally on your device in this version of Ledgerly. They are not uploaded to our cloud database.

Offline and local data. The app caches account and expense information on your device so you can use it offline. This may include local account profiles, cached expenses, categories, preferences, and local app settings.

Technical information. Our service providers may process technical information such as your IP address, device or browser type, request timestamps, and diagnostic logs to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Google sign-in. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive an authentication token and the basic profile information needed to create or access your Ledgerly account. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive, or other Google services.

Website. The Ledgerly website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address and browser information when those fonts are requested. The Ledgerly app does not use advertising cookies.

2. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate, and maintain your Ledgerly account.
  • Store, sync, and display your expenses, budgets, savings goals, and settings.
  • Provide offline access and restore your data when you reconnect.
  • Protect the service, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Respond to your support requests and comply with legal obligations.
  • Maintain and improve Ledgerly features.

We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. How we share information

We share information only as needed to operate Ledgerly:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and hosting services. Cloud data is protected by access controls and row-level security so that each signed-in user can access only their own records through the app.
  • Google provides Google sign-in and Google Fonts. Google processes information under its own privacy policy when you use those services.
  • Service providers may process information on our behalf for hosting, security, and technical operations, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • Legal and safety disclosures may be made if required by law or if necessary to protect our rights, users, or the public.
  • Business transfer may involve transferring information as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

4. Google user data

Ledgerly's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We request only the basic Google account information needed to sign you in. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain your cloud account and expense data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your account and associated cloud data within 30 days, except for information we are required to retain by law or for security and backup purposes.

To request deletion, email us at jonhobayan16@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Ledgerly account. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request.

You can delete local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. This does not delete data stored in your Ledgerly cloud account. Deleting your Ledgerly account does not delete your Google account.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, access controls, and database row-level security. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You can update your name and budget in the app. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at jonhobayan16@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children's privacy

Ledgerly is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. We take reasonable steps to protect information in accordance with this policy and applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page and change the date above. If we make material changes, we may also notify you in the app or on the website. Continued use of Ledgerly after an update means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, contact us at jonhobayan16@gmail.com. Please include "Privacy" in the subject line so we can route your request.
